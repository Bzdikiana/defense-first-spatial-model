Continuous or discrete?

In time: continuous

Every number here is recomputed five times a second for the whole possession, not once per pass or per zone entry. For a possession-level summary the model takes the moment of greatest danger: the defense's cover at that moment predicts shots, while cover averaged over the possession does not.

In space: a continuous field, counted in zones

Pitch control and the danger maps are smooth functions of position, evaluated on 2 m cells. The zones view adds the same cells into the 18 classic zones, so the danger left open is identical either way; zones only change how it is read.

The danger maps

Shooting danger (xG from here) is a smooth function of distance and angle to goal. Possession value (xT) is estimated on 12 × 8 zones of the pitch and smoothed between their centres, so it spreads danger across midfield. Switch maps to compare: the defense's cover predicts shots with either once it is measured separately from where the ball is.

Data: SkillCorner open data (A-League 2024/25), broadcast tracking. Pitch control fitted to the open data; danger in xG; chance of a shot from the two-part model in dsv.defense. Attack always plays left to right here. Code: scripts/13_explorer.py, package dsv.
